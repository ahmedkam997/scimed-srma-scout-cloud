Discovery pipeline

  1. Generate review-topic candidates
  2. Rank candidates
  3. Search competing published reviews
  4. Search PROSPERO and OSF registrations
  5. Discover primary-study candidates
  6. Check ongoing/future evidence when relevant
  7. Deduplicate candidate studies
  8. Assess novelty and redundancy
  9. Apply discovery qualification gates
  10. Export detailed research dossiers and ready-to-use ChatGPT verification prompts for each topic
  11. Research Gap Verification: prioritize unresolved competitors and document source-linked manual findings without changing scientific qualification gates
  12. Final Evidence Audit: compare competing protocols, published reviews and study families before any GO/REFINE/NO-GO recommendation

A failed source is never interpreted as a negative search.
