# Independent PICO audit: LAAO versus DOACs in dialysis-dependent ESRD

**Audit date:** 24 November 2026  
**SciMed candidate:** CAND-BCD199B816F41CFC  
**Discovery job:** 4d10637b-b367-474c-b588-770b5834764b  
**Decision:** **NO-GO for immediate registration as a fully feasible new DOAC-only dialysis meta-analysis. REFINE/UNRESOLVED if an independently screened update yields four or more non-overlapping comparable populations with extractable DOAC-specific effects.**

This audit is a source-grounded *triage*, not a completed systematic review, validated exhaustive search, full-text extraction, or risk-of-bias evaluation. Search dates and study eligibility must be confirmed from each complete publication and supplementary material.

## Exact target PICO

- **Population:** Adults with non-valvular atrial fibrillation and *dialysis-dependent* ESRD/ESKD (hemodialysis or peritoneal dialysis). Mixed CKD 4–5 cohorts are not sufficient without extractable dialysis-specific data.
- **Intervention:** Percutaneous left atrial appendage occlusion/closure (LAAO/LAAC).
- **Comparator:** *Direct oral anticoagulants* (DOACs; apixaban, rivaroxaban, dabigatran, or edoxaban), not an inseparable mixture of DOAC and warfarin users.
- **Outcomes:** Ischemic stroke/systemic embolism, major bleeding, mortality, procedure harms. Require compatible comparative effects/events and explicit denominators/time horizons.
- **Design:** RCTs and comparative observational cohorts, reported separately; do not infer causal superiority from propensity-matched data.

### Confirmed number of independent and extractable populations

**Unknown, not zero.** At least one directly relevant observational *report* (Al-Abcha 2025) exists, but its independence from other USRDS reports and outcome-specific extraction remain unaudited. No set of four independent, dialysis-specific, DOAC-comparator clinical cohorts with verified extractable outcomes was established by this audit. Do not equate bibliographic search hits, multiple papers or conference abstracts with four independent studies. No dialysis-specific LAAO-versus-DOAC RCT was verified.

## Strong competing reviews and registrations

1. **JACC conference meta-analysis (April 2026)** ([DOI 10.1016/j.jacc.2026.02.129](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacc.2026.02.129)): LAAO versus *mixed OAC* among ESKD + AF; **three observational studies (n=2747)**, last search **September 2025**. Overlaps in population and intervention but does not establish DOAC-only pooling.
2. **European Stroke Journal conference meta-analysis (May 2026)** ([full abstract](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13146243/)): LAAO versus OAC among ESRD + AF, **22,512 total**, search through **December 2025**. Reports subgroup results in studies *predominantly* using DOACs. This is a strong overlap signal, but a predominance subgroup is **not** proof of a pure DOAC group or independent eligible populations.
3. **2025 individual patient-level meta-analysis** ([PMCID PMC12548373](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12548373/)): 17 studies including 1047 ESRD recipients. Main question was ESRD versus non-ESRD among LAAO patients; **not an equivalent direct LAAO-versus-DOAC meta-analysis**.
4. [PROSPERO 1371399](https://www.crd.york.ac.uk/PROSPERO/view/1371399) addresses LAAO versus medical treatment, identifies severe renal failure/dialysis and DOACs; potential overlap, but protocol-specific inclusion/exclusion and registration status require independent inspection.
5. **LAA-KIDNEY NCT05204212** described as an ongoing trial in the 2025 JACC paper. Do **not** classify the unpublished future trial as evidence of existing RCT extractability. Reconfirm live trial status before planning an update.

- If multiple reports share an underlying population, choose the most directly comparable adjusted endpoint/time horizon per population and document choices.

## Research decision and next action

**Do not register the original idea now as a novel, adequately powered dialysis-only DOAC meta-analysis.** Reasons: overlapping 2026 reviews, DOAC-specific comparative cohort uncertainty, multiple candidate publications with overlapping populations, and no verified four independent extractable clinical populations.

**Conditional REFINE:** Consider a methodologically distinct *living evidence map / review of comparative stroke-prevention modalities in ESKD with separate DOAC and VKA nodes* only after a fresh novelty audit against the 2026 JACC and ESOC reviews. A new quantitative meta-analysis needs independent non-overlapping populations and a clinically meaningful new study or subgroup.
