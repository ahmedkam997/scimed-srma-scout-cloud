## Prespecified direct-accuracy eligibility rule

A study is **currently usable** for the originally proposed DTA synthesis only if all are verified: (1) adults with *intermediate* left-main coronary stenosis, with a separately identifiable LM lesion denominator; (2) coronary CCTA-derived CT-FFR performed for the LM target; (3) paired **invasive pressure-wire fractional flow reserve** measuring the relevant LM lesion, not visual ICA stenosis or a global composite anatomical endpoint; (4) compatible thresholds (document both, usually 0.80) and measurement locations in LM/LAD/LCx; (5) LM-only **TP, FP, FN and TN** or source data sufficient to reconstruct them; (6) an independent study family with patient/vessel/lesion unit and potential multiple-lesion clustering accounted for. A partial-data study remains pending even if general coronary accuracy is high.

A four-study minimum is SciMed's feasibility screening target, **not** a universal minimum in the Cochrane DTA methodology. Neither sensitivity/specificity for all vessels nor an anatomical LM subgroup is a valid LM-specific invasive-FFR 2×2.

### Audited count and denominator caution

This table contains **11 bibliographic report groups or citation leads** (the last line includes two separate new studies). Several were inspected at full-text level, some only via abstract/searchable indexed article portions. **Zero LM-specific, independently verified, invasive-wire-FFR 2×2 study populations emerged from the sources inspected at the required level of detail.** This is a statement about the **bounded screen**, **not** an estimate of the total eligible-study count in all databases. The global `eligible_independent_study_count` therefore remains `null`/unknown.

Importantly, the 2021 AccuFFRct study provides **one valid all-coronary-vessel 2×2** (TP20, FP6, FN2, TN50). Those counts **must not be reused as if they were Left-Main-only data**.

### Independent-family notes

- The 2021 Riedl left-main clinical-utility study should not be pooled with an arbitrary Aarhus patient cohort until dates and source population are reconciled.
- The 2026 Dan-NICAD III post-hoc analysis must be linked to the parent trial cohort; an individual LM error is not an independent study.
- The two 2026 prospective/multicenter articles above were retained only as potential sources of supplementary LM subgroup data; no verified patient overlap or independence was inferred.
- Left main disease is not synonymous with proximal LAD disease. Reports combining `LM/LAD` or `left coronary tree` should be assigned **EXTRACTION_PENDING**, not automatically included.

## Existing syntheses and conceptual overlap

- [2024 agreement meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11179792/): 43 reports, 5,236 patients, 7,291 vessels; **general CAD**, not verified separate intermediate-LM DTA. This makes a broad CT-FFR-vs-wire-FFR meta-analysis highly duplicative.
- [2025 ML CT-FFR accuracy synthesis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11795686/): broad coronary accuracy, not independently verified exact LM PICO.
- [2026 SCCT/SCAI CT-FFR consensus](https://www.journalofcardiovascularct.com/article/S1934-5925%2826%2900101-2/fulltext): explicitly says not to use CT-FFR to determine left-main functional significance because LM thresholds/downstream LAD/LCx pressure-drop interpretation require validation. This is a rationale for further LM validation research, **not evidence of existing multiple cohorts**.
- PROSPERO, OSF and all original search sources remain only **partially assessed**. No registration-free/novelty-cleared status is claimed.

## Actionable decision

**Do not begin statistical DTA meta-analysis or register the exact LM-only diagnostic-accuracy PICO yet.** The most important blocker is unavailable LM-specific paired wire-FFR outcome data, not the lack of a meta-analysis software package.

1. Request subgroup 2×2 data and invasive reference definitions from the AccuFFRct 2021 investigators and Dan-NICAD III 2026 authors, including thresholds, lesion-level denominators and patient identifiers (de-identified stable IDs if feasible).
2. Check full text/supplementary material of the new 2026 morphology/long-segment studies for true LM inclusion. If present, obtain LM-only 2×2.
3. Screen PROSPERO/OSF completely and cross-check the broad 2024/2025 reviews for LM subgroups; report actual last search dates only when documented.
4. If no independently extractable LM wire-FFR populations materialize, **close this specific SRMA candidate as not feasible at present**. A transparent *evidence-gap map or scoping review of LM-specific CT-FFR validation limitations* is a different question requiring its own novelty audit and protocol.

For future quantitative analysis, compare [MetaDTA open-source](https://github.com/CRSU-Apps/MetaDTA) and Cochrane DTA bivariate/HSROC methods; do not generate a model from pooled general-CAD studies to fill LM gaps.

## Research process and limits

This was a **targeted full-text and abstract feasibility audit**, using exact LM, CT-FFR, invasive wire FFR and anatomical-reference terms, plus recent 2026 reports and primary methodological documents. The search was not exported as a reproducible, exhaustive PubMed/Embase/Scopus/PROSPERO/OSF result set with PRISMA deduplication. Titles, abstracts and records not retrieved in the bounded search might alter feasibility. Findings require independent second-reviewer confirmation before final exclusion decisions. Original cloud job and screening/qualification gates were not changed.
